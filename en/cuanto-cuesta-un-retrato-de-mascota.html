How much does a hand-painted pet portrait cost?

Every portrait is an original work hand-painted on wood. These are the prices in Argentine pesos (ARS):

  • Small · 25x35 cm · ARS 280,000
  • Medium · 35x45 cm · ARS 390,000
  • Large · 50x70 cm · ARS 490,000

Each price includes the Kiri wood frame with glass, rear backing and hanging hardware, special packaging and free shipping across Argentina. The Box frame has an extra cost of ARS 10,000.

To reserve, a 50% deposit is paid, and the balance when the portrait is finished. Production takes 10 to 15 business days, and home delivery adds 5 to 7 business days.
