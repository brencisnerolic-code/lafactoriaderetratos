Their gaze, forever in your home

Losing a pet hurts in a way that isn't always understood. A hand-painted portrait doesn't replace them, but it can be a place where their gaze stays with you.

We work calmly, starting from your photos and from what you tell us about them, and we review the painting with you before calling it finished. Each portrait is an original work. You can message us on WhatsApp whenever you like: no rush and no commitment.
