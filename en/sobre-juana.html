Behind every portrait is an artist

La Factoría is an author-portrait studio created by Juana Perfecta, an Argentine visual artist. We make hyperrealist portraits hand-painted by her, in a practice that combines painting and the exploration of materials.

Each portrait is painted personally by her, from start to finish, based on the photographs and story of each pet.

Juana Perfecta is the artistic name of Brenda Cisnero. She holds a degree in Visual Arts, has more than ten years of teaching experience, and has trained and worked as an artist in Argentina and the United States.
